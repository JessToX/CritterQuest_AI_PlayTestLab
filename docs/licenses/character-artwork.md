## 成品来源 / Artwork provenance

随游戏提供的是为 Critter Quest 绘制并由 Jess 采纳的十款成品。八款来自牌桌角色定稿，红狐与座头鲸来自各自单独审定的 A 版。浣熊与红狐从空白绘制。物种资料仅供形态观察。每款用两张静态 SVG 表现同一道具的动作与收势，待命时由界面加灰度。

These finished portraits were drawn for Critter Quest and selected by Jess. Eight pairs come from the tabletop character series. The red fox and humpback use their individually approved design A. The raccoon and red fox were drawn from scratch. Species references informed anatomy. Each pair depicts a table action and its resting pose in two static SVGs. The interface applies grayscale to the resting pose.
